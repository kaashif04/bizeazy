# Surface: BizEazy Hub (app shell + Hub overview; modules inherit)

Mode: Operate. Owner, family admins and office staff; phone first (quick checks and real work between rushes), desktop for month-end. First glance must give: money (owed, collected, this month), what needs me today, quick actions. Must not feel: cramped, flashy neon, childish, or bland bank/government. Printed documents (`data-document`, branch templates) untouched. Snappy above all: no waiting that the network does not force.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every figure reads like a payment-success screen, an amount you trust at a glance, confirmed with a tick. Refuses the category default of grey stat cards in a white grid with a sidebar of icons.

OWN-WORLD: A committed blue money field (#1463FF) that owns the top of the Hub and the active nav; cool paper ground (#F4F6FB) and pure white surfaces; deep navy ink (#0B1B3F), never black; mint (#00C48C) means paid/done, amber means waiting, coral-red means late. Oversized tabular figures in one geometric workhorse face (Manrope), heavy for amounts, medium for labels. Big rounded cards (20px), pill buttons and status pills that always pair a dot with a word. The receipt edge: the money field ends in a scalloped tear line, the one ornament the world allows.

STORY: The owner opens the app, sees in one breath what came in and what is owed this month, sees the two or three things that need them today ranked by urgency, and acts with one tap from the thumb.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone 390 wide. Top third: the blue field with the branch name small, "Collected this month" label, the RM figure at ~44px heavy white, then a white sub-row: Owed RM, Invoiced RM, and a thin bar of collected over invoiced; the field ends in the scalloped edge. Directly under it, quick actions as a row of four round white action buttons with labels (Invoice, Quote, Payslip, Reports) overlapping the field's edge. Then "Needs you" as a list of rounded rows, each a coloured dot, a sentence and a days-left pill. Desktop: the field becomes a wide band with the three figures side by side; needs-you and recent activity sit in two columns below.

FORM: Payment Confirmed (e-wallet/DuitNow payment-success screens), my top-ranked grounded candidate (#1 of 7), chosen by the user as IMPECCABLE'S PICK on re-roll 1, seed key c27bf94f. Signature interaction: amounts count up in 300ms on arrival and a confirming tick draws itself when something is paid or saved. Motion grammar: 150–220ms ease-out, transform and opacity only, nothing blocks input, reduced motion honoured.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
